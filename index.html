FIELD MANUAL

Think one row ahead.

You are blue. Each round, choose a key within two squares of your jelly. Everyone jumps together, while the icons shift down one row. To collect an icon, land on the key below its current position.

You have 15 / 12 / 8 seconds on Easy / Medium / Hard. Click to select, then press Space to jump early, or wait for the timer. Arrow keys move your selection. A Jelly Off rolls 1–10 for each contender. The loser takes 15 HP damage and is knocked to a neighboring key; falling off is fatal.

Last jelly standing wins. This rebuild uses approximate rules and new artwork; it does not restore online multiplayer.
